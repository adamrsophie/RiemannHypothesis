The Riemann Hypothesis, formalized in Lean

On 20 November 2026 Levent Alpöge released a Lean 4 project of 175,187 files that claims a complete, assumption free proof of the Riemann Hypothesis and of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis for Dirichlet L-functions. This site explains what the project states, how its top level is put together, how it is organized, and how anyone can check it.

What is claimed

The Riemann Hypothesis

Every nontrivial zero of the Riemann zeta function \(\zeta(s)\) has real part \(\tfrac12\). Stated with Mathlib's own RiemannHypothesis.

A zero free half plane

\(L(s,\chi) \neq 0\) whenever \(\operatorname{Re} s > \tfrac12\), for every Dirichlet character \(\chi\), apart from the pole of \(\zeta\) at \(s=1\).

GRH for Dirichlet L-functions

Every zero of \(L(s,\chi)\) in the critical strip \(0 < \operatorname{Re} s < 1\) lies on the line \(\operatorname{Re} s = \tfrac12\), for every Dirichlet character.

Provenance

The lean/ directory of the repository is the unmodified archive lean.tar.gz that Alpöge distributed in 14 parts through the first footnote of remark.pdf. Its git tree hash is e517b335d21898fd2506ad538a1e1775c0aaa4e4, identical to the lean/ tree of the independent mirror jsmorph/rh, so the two copies are byte for byte the same.
