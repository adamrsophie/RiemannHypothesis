The announcement

  • Alpöge writes that he believes he has, working with Claude, discovered a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, that the method "easily extends" to the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis, and that he expects the Extended Riemann Hypothesis with more time.
  • He states that the arguments have been formalized in Lean without assumptions, and that the formalization is finished.
  • He describes Claude as having "far outpaced" him near the end, and says he guided the work toward the right analogue of a toy setting.
  • He expects the argument to be "massively simplified", plans to write it up properly, and chose to release only the Lean code. A machine generated exposition would exceed 250,000 pages, which he calls inappropriate to post.

The central idea

Stepanov's method and multiplicity

Stepanov's 1969 proof of the Riemann Hypothesis for curves over finite fields constructs an auxiliary function that vanishes to high order (multiplicity) at every rational point of the curve. Comparing its degree with the number of zeros counted with multiplicity bounds the number of points, which is the Riemann Hypothesis for the curve. The note calls the new argument "a descendant of Stepanov's".

The obstacle over \(\mathbb{Z}\) is that multiplicity "corresponds to something like differentiating whole numbers". There is no derivative in the integer direction.

Chains of theta lifts

Fourier analytic ideas in a toy setting led to chaining theta lifts: transferring automorphic forms along a sequence of groups

$$U(X_0) \to U(X_1) \to \cdots \to U(X_m)$$

between totally definite unitary groups, where each lift plays the role of one more order of vanishing. The note's highlighted line: for a von Mangoldt sum of length \(X\), the suggested length of the chain is \(m \approx \sqrt{X}\). In the note's words, "And yet, what else could it be?"

Named ingredients

The note mentions, among others: a Stepanov type determinant; positivity via determinants; the explicit formula of Guinand and Weil, which supplies a sign; Gan-Gross-Prasad periods; the Chowla-Selberg formula; Weyl's law; the Siegel mass formula; Iwasawa towers; a seesaw identity identifying the period of the iterated lift with a Bessel period (Rallis); the Bost slope inequality from Arakelov geometry; a relative trace formula; a weakened form of Vandiver's conjecture; and Behrend's construction of sets without arithmetic progressions.

Timeline

The note lists 20 steps, provided by Claude after a search of the work, all in 2026. They are shown in the note's order (which is not strictly chronological), with brief paraphrases.

  • 6 Oct#1
    Stepanov determinant.
  • 9 Oct#2
    An invariant derivation \(D\) on a CM curve, with \(D^p \equiv 0\) for primes \(p\) inert in the CM field, and high divisibility by \(p\) of iterated derivatives of a theta value.
  • 19 Oct onward#3
    An identity \(qN = M^k + R\) built from the "path" quadric \(x_1x_2 + \cdots + x_{k-1}x_k\), seeking automorphy via theta functions on a sixfold cover of \(\mathrm{GL}_5\).
  • 6 Oct onward#4
    Positivity \(W(1_S) \ge 0\) via a determinant, read as Stepanov with multiplicity one; then bootstrap.
  • 20 Oct#5
    Switch definitively to a weighted von Mangoldt sum; Guinand-Weil gives the sign; a variance test function trick.
  • 8 Oct#6
    A positivity inequality for \(W\) applied to indicator functions, which the note says "gives \(A positivity statement giving a zero free region beyond \(1 - \eta\).gt; 1 - \eta\)".
  • 14 Oct#7
    Determinants for weights containing the desired GGP periods, with interlacing and non interlacing weights.
  • 15 Nov#8
    Iterated theta lifts between totally definite unitary groups, \(\pi \mapsto \pi \boxplus \mu_j[t]\), winning multiplicity \(m\). Take \(m\) like \(\sqrt X\).
  • 15 Nov#9
    Norms of the lifts, level, and cancellation of order \(m\) terms, with divisibility via Chowla-Selberg, Weyl and the Siegel mass formula.
  • 15 Nov#10
    A sufficient lower bound on the divisibility of the \(m\) fold iterated lift.
  • 6 Oct#11
    An Iwasawa tower, by analogy with pigeonhole over \(\mathbb F_{q^n}/\mathbb F_q\).
  • 15 Nov#12
    Seesaw: the period of the iterated lift is a Bessel period independent of the Hecke characters (Rallis).
  • 6 Oct#13
    Bost slope inequality.
  • 15 Nov#14
    Precise cost of each lift; a priori one would stop at exponent 2/3, so a further idea ("division") is needed.
  • 15 Nov#15
    An integral structure glued across Hecke characters, giving a "gcd", that is, extra averaging.
  • 14 Oct#16
    A relative trace formula, reducing by Cauchy-Schwarz to a norm bound for a projected Poincaré series; approximate orthogonality.
  • 15 Nov#17
    For Galois cohomology classes that twisting cannot kill, a significant weakening of Vandiver's conjecture.
  • 15 Nov#18
    Covolumes, the index of theta series, and congruences between lifts and forms.
  • 19 Oct#19
    The precise choice of Iwasawa tower, using Behrend sets.
